1. Problem Identifying

Chợ Tốt's core value is to ensure that pro-sellers' listings are shown to the right buyers at the right time - increasing the chance of meaningful connections between serious buyers and professional sellers, ultimately leading to more leads and closed deals.

    • Reduce manual workload for pro-sellers (e.g., no need to manually schedule bumping time)

2. Ad Quality:

  • Improve the content of listings (price, images, descriptions) to attract the right buyers

3. Ad Performance Dashboard:

  • A business tool to help sellers manage a large number of listings more effectively, based on performance data

Key AI-Powered Features

  • Full Automation with AI: Leverages AI to automate the entire optimization process - enabling full performance improvement with just one click.
  • Proactive & Automated Guidance: Automatically sends daily performance summaries and smart recommendations (promote ads, content edits), removing the need for manual tracking and interpretation.
